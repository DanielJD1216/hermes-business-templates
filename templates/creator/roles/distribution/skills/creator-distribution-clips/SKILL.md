# Clips

**Trigger:** the final cut is approved.

## Steps
1. Pick 3 to 5 moments that stand alone.
2. Give each an in and out time, a hook line and a cover that follows canon/brand.md.

## Done when
The clip list is in review.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
