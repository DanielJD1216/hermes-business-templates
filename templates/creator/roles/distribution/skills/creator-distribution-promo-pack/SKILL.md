# Promo pack

**Trigger:** a long-form video is published.

## Steps
1. Write one post per platform the owner uses, each with a different angle, in the owner's voice.
2. Follow the platform rules in canon/standing.md.

## Done when
The pack is in review.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
