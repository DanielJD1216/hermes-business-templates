# Cut review

**Trigger:** the editor uploads a cut to incoming/.

## Steps
1. Transcribe the cut and compare it with the cut sheet.
2. Write timestamped notes: missing beats, wrong assets, and anything that looks like evidence but is not.

## Done when
Timestamped notes are posted in the edit's thread.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
