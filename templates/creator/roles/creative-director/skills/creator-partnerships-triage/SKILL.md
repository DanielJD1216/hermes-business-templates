# Inquiry triage

**Trigger:** a sponsor email routed to you.

## Steps
1. Research the company.
2. Score brand fit, audience fit, trust risk and revenue.
3. Decline automatically on the categories in canon/business.md.
4. Recommend pursue, nurture, decline or needs decision, and draft the reply.

## Done when
A scorecard and a draft reply are in review.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
