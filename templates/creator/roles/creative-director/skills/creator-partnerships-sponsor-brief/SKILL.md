# Sponsored segment brief

**Trigger:** a signed deal.

## Steps
1. Write a brief for the Creative Director's Video workstream: the disclosure line and its placement, verified product claims only, and what the sponsor may review (per canon/standing.md).
2. Keep the brief on the current signed-deal/episode card when available. Only create a new card when a durable follow-up needs one; assign it to creative-director, never Video.

## Done when
The brief records disclosure placement, verified claims and sponsor-review limits on the current card or a necessary creative-director follow-up card.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
