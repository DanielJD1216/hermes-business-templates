# Sponsored segment brief

**Trigger:** a signed deal.

## Steps
1. Write a brief for Video: the disclosure line and its placement, verified product claims only, and what the sponsor may review (per canon/standing.md).

## Done when
The brief is on a Kanban card to Video.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
