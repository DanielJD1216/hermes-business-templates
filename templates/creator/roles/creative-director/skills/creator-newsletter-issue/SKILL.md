# Video to issue

**Trigger:** a published card from Video.

## Steps
1. Pick the one idea that works best in writing.
2. Draft the issue. Never paste a transcript.
3. Link the video once.

## Done when
The draft and its source map are in the issue folder.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
