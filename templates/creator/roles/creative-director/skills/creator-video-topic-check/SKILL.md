# Topic check

**Trigger:** a new idea or a topic-queue item.

## Steps
1. Write the one wrong belief the video corrects, in the viewer's own words.
2. Find at least 2 similar videos from other channels that beat their own channel's usual views.
3. Confirm the topic fits the lane in canon/business.md.

## Done when
A go or no-go note sits in the episode folder with the belief sentence and both references.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
