# Schedule

**Trigger:** `approve` in the thread.

## Steps
1. Schedule in the owner's tool. If the tool is not connected, write the exact steps for the owner and label them draft-and-paste.
2. Read the schedule back and confirm the time, account and media.

## Done when
The confirmation link or ID is recorded, and any failure is flagged in the thread.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
