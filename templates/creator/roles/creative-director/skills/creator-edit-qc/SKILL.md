# Pre-publish QC

**Trigger:** the final cut.

## Steps
1. Check every on-screen number against the source sheet.
2. Check that any sponsor disclosure comes first.
3. Check the standing outro against canon/standing.md.
4. Check that nothing plays before the opening hook.
5. Check the house style rules in canon/standing.md.

## Done when
A pass or fail list exists, with a timestamp for every fail.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
