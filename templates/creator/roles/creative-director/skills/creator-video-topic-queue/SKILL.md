# Topic queue from comments

**Trigger:** the monthly card from Ops with the raw comment pull.

## Steps
1. Group the comments by theme and tag the belief behind each theme.
2. Keep a word-for-word list of one-off comments that fit no group.

## Done when
Topic-queue.md is updated and dated.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
