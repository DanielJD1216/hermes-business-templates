# Research and source sheet

**Trigger:** the topic is approved.

## Steps
1. Research the topic.
2. List every claim that will appear on screen with its URL and the exact sentence behind it.
3. Mark each claim as screen-record, text card or unresolved.

## Done when
The source sheet has zero unmarked claims.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
