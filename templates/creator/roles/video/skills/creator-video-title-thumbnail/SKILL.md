# Title and thumbnail

**Trigger:** the script is approved.

## Steps
1. Score at least 5 titles, using the core-point line as the baseline.
2. Make the title and the thumbnail say different things.
3. Check both at phone size beside the videos they will compete with.

## Done when
Ranked options with scores are in review, and the winner beats the baseline.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
