# Post for a new video

**Trigger:** a video is published.

## Steps
1. Pick a route: feed post plus lesson (lasting fundamentals), feed post only (timely), or skip (sponsored or promotional, per canon/standing.md).
2. Draft for the chosen route.

## Done when
The route and the draft are in review.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
