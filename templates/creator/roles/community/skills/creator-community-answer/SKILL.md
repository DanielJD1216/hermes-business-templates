# Member answers

**Trigger:** a new member question, pasted in or pulled by Ops.

## Steps
1. Draft an answer from the owner's own material and link the video or lesson it comes from.
2. If nothing covers it, say so and flag it as a topic.

## Done when
The draft and its source are in the thread.

## Approval
Post the result in its own thread and move the card to review. Nothing leaves the business without the owner's `approve`.

The Kanban board is named in your AGENTS.md. Read canon/ before any public-facing work.
